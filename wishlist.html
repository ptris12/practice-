Simple Tiramisu Cake Recipe

Ingredients

  • 250g mascarpone
  • 200ml heavy cream
  • 3 tbsp sugar
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 1 cup strong coffee (cooled)
  • 1 pack ladyfingers
  • Cocoa powder for topping

Steps

  1. Whip heavy cream with sugar and vanilla until soft peaks form.
  2. Fold in mascarpone until smooth and creamy.
  3. Quickly dip each ladyfinger in cooled coffee and layer in a dish.
  4. Spread half of the cream mixture over the first layer.
  5. Repeat with another layer of dipped ladyfingers and the remaining cream.
  6. Dust generously with cocoa powder and chill for at least 4 hours.
